Nathan 14 Assistive Technology Device

Nathan 14 is an assistive technology device designed to support students with low vision in education. It combines a tablet, distance-viewing camera, adjustable stand and accessible software in an integrated system.

The device is designed to help students view classroom whiteboards, access learning materials and work more independently in the classroom.

  • 14-inch Android tablet display.
  • 4K distance camera with optical zoom for viewing classroom boards.
  • Adjustable stand designed for classroom use.
  • Accessible controls and supporting peripherals.
  • Software tools to support reading, learning and schoolwork.

Nathan 14 Scientific Calculator

The Nathan 14 Scientific Calculator, also known as ExamCalc, is an accessible scientific calculator designed with visually impaired students in mind. It supports mathematical learning through an accessible digital interface.
