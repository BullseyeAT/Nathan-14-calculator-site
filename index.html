The Nathan 14 Scientific Calculator is a powerful and reliable calculator app designed specifically for students preparing for Leaving Certificate Higher Level, GCSE Maths, and other secondary school or college-level exams. Perfect for homework, practice, and final exams, this app brings the full capabilities of a physical scientific calculator straight to your device seamlessly.

  • Comprehensive scientific calculator functions: standard deviation, linear regression, trigonometry, logarithms, exponentials, powers, roots, fractions, percentages, and more.
  • Supports all functions required for LC HL Maths and GCSE Mathematics.
  • Easy-to-use interface for quick calculations during study sessions or exams.

Unique Exam-Ready Features

  • Integrated Formula Tables: Access commonly used Maths, Physics and Chemistry formulas without leaving the app.
  • Built-in Logarithm Tables: Quickly reference log values for manual calculations or revision.

Nathan 14 Assistive Technology Device

The Nathan 14 Assistive Technology Device is designed to make advanced mathematics accessible for students with visual impairments and other accessibility needs. It combines powerful software with inclusive design to ensure students can perform at their full potential in exams and classroom environments.

  • Improves accessibility and independence for students
  • Designed with exam compliance in mind
  • Supports efficient learning and revision
  • Helps bridge the gap between ability and opportunity
